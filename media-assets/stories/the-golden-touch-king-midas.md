# The Golden Touch (King Midas)
*A Classic Legend*

Long ago, King Midas ruled over a peaceful kingdom. He loved roses, his garden, and above all, his sweet young daughter, Marigold.

However, King Midas also loved gold. He spent hours counting his gold coins in his treasure vault.

One day, a mysterious golden spirit appeared and granted the king a single wish.
"I wish that everything I touch would turn to pure gold!" exclaimed Midas greedily.

The spirit smiled. "Your wish is granted."

The next morning, Midas touched his wooden bedpost—it turned instantly to shining gold! He touched an apple from the bowl—it turned to heavy gold. He touched a red rose in the garden—its soft petals stiffened into cold, metallic gold.

Midas sat down for breakfast, but when he tried to drink water, it turned to liquid gold. When he touched a warm roll of bread, it hardened into solid gold! He could not eat or drink.

His little daughter Marigold ran into the room and hugged him. To his horror, the sweet girl turned into a cold, motionless golden statue!

Midas fell to his knees, weeping. "Take away this terrible curse! Gold is worthless compared to my precious daughter!"

The spirit took pity on Midas and told him to wash in the sacred river waters. Midas filled a pitcher of river water and sprinkled it over his daughter and the garden.

Instantly, Marigold smiled and breathed again, running into her father's arms. The roses bloomed with sweet fragrance once more. King Midas never cared for gold again.
