# The Wind and the Sun
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

High above the clouds, the North Wind and the Golden Sun were having a lively conversation about who was the stronger of the two.

"I can blow down giant trees and churn the ocean waves!" roared the Wind proudly.

"Strength comes in many forms," the Sun answered with a gentle smile.

Down below on a winding dirt path, a traveler walked along wearing a thick wool cloak.

"Let us see," proposed the Wind. "Whoever can make that traveler take off his cloak first is the strongest!"

The Wind took a deep breath, puffed up his cheeks, and blew with all his fury: *WHOOOOOSH!*

The cold gusts tore leaves from branches. But the harder the Wind blew, the tighter the traveler wrapped his warm cloak around his shoulders.

At last, the Wind grew tired and stopped.

Now it was the Sun's turn. The Sun stepped out from behind a fluffy white cloud and beamed warm, golden rays down upon the traveler.

The air grew pleasantly warm. The traveler unbuttoned his collar. The Sun shone brighter and warmer still.

Soon, feeling the joyful heat, the traveler smiled, took off his heavy cloak, and rested comfortably in the pleasant shade of a leafy tree.

The Wind sighed and tipped his hat. The gentle warmth of the Sun had won.
