# The Ugly Duckling
*By Hans Christian Andersen (1843)*

It was summer in the country. The wheat was golden, the oats were green, and deep in the yellow hay near the pond, a mother duck sat patiently on her nest.

One by one, the eggs cracked open: *peep, peep!* Out tumbled fluffy little yellow ducklings.

Only one large egg remained. At last it cracked, and out stepped a big, awkward grey bird with long legs.

"He does not look like the others," quacked the other farm birds, teasing him unkindly.

Feeling lonely and sad, the little grey bird wandered away from the farmyard to a secluded marsh. All through the cold autumn and snowy winter, he endured the icy winds and freezing waters.

When gentle spring returned, the sun shone warm again. The bird spread his wings and felt a surprising surge of power and grace. He flew down toward a sparkling blue lake.

Three majestic white swans swam gracefully upon the water. The bird bowed his head in admiration. Looking down into the clear glassy surface, he saw his own reflection.

He was no longer an awkward grey bird—he had grown into a beautiful, graceful white swan!

Children ran down to the bank and threw breadcrumbs into the water, cheering: "Look at the magnificent new swan! He is the loveliest of all!"
