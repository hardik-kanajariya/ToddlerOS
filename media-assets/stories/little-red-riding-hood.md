# Little Red Riding Hood
*A Classic Fairy Tale*

Once upon a time, there was a sweet young girl loved by everyone in the village. Her grandmother gave her a pretty little hood of red velvet, which she wore so often that everyone called her Little Red Riding Hood.

One sunny afternoon, her mother packed a wicker basket with fresh bread, golden butter, and sweet honey cakes.

"Take this basket to Grandmother's cottage in the woods," said her mother. "Stay on the main forest path and do not stray into the deep woods."

Red Riding Hood promised and skipped along the sun-dappled path. Along the way, she stopped to smell purple wildflowers and listen to the bluebirds singing.

A sly wolf saw her and asked politely, "Where are you going on this fine day, little girl?"

"I am visiting my grandmother who lives in the cozy cottage by the three tall oak trees," she replied cheerfully.

The clever wolf ran ahead, but a friendly forest woodsman was nearby cutting firewood. Seeing the wolf sneak toward the cottage, the woodsman hurried over and chased the wolf far away into the distant mountains.

When Little Red Riding Hood arrived, she hugged her grandmother and shared the sweet honey cakes. She thanked the kind woodsman and learned always to keep to the safe, sunny path.
