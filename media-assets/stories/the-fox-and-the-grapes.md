# The Fox and the Grapes
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

One warm autumn afternoon, a sleek red fox trotted through a sunlit vineyard.

He looked up and saw thick clusters of deep purple grapes hanging from a high wooden arbor. The grapes were plump and shiny, glistening with morning dew.

"Those sweet grapes look delicious," said the fox, his mouth watering.

The fox took a running start, jumped high into the air, and snapped his jaws. But he missed the grapes by several inches.

He stepped back, ran again, and leaped with all his might: *whoosh!* Missed again.

A third time he jumped, reaching as high as his paws could stretch, but the grapes remained safely out of reach on the tall vine.

Finally, tired and out of breath, the fox held his chin high and turned his nose up in the air.

"I didn't want those grapes anyway," muttered the fox as he walked away. "I am sure they are sour!"

The little birds watching from the branches chirped with amusement. They knew the grapes were sweet as honey!
