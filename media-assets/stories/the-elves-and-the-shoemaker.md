# The Elves and the Shoemaker
*A Classic Brothers Grimm Fairy Tale*

In a quiet village, an honest shoemaker and his wife worked very hard, but through no fault of their own, they became very poor. At last, he had only enough leather left to make one single pair of shoes.

That evening, he carefully cut out the leather on his workbench, said his evening prayers, and went peacefully to sleep.

In the morning, to his astonishment, standing upon the workbench was a pair of the most beautiful, perfectly stitched shoes he had ever seen! A customer bought them immediately for a handsome price.

With the money, the shoemaker bought leather for two more pairs. Again, he left the cut leather out overnight, and in the morning, two magnificent pairs of shoes were waiting.

One night near winter, the shoemaker and his wife hid behind a curtain to see who their secret helpers were.

At midnight, two tiny elves leaped through the window. With nimble fingers, they stitched and hammered: *tap, tap, tap!* until all the shoes were done.

"These little friends have helped us so much," said the shoemaker's wife. "Let us make them warm little coats, trousers, and tiny shoes to keep out the winter cold!"

The next night, instead of leather, the table held two tiny warm outfits. The elves jumped with joy, put on their fine clothes, and danced happily out into the moonlight, leaving the kind shoemaker prosperous forever.
