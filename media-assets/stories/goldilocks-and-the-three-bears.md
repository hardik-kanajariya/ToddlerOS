# Goldilocks and the Three Bears
*A Traditional Fairy Tale*

Once upon a time, deep in a green forest, lived three friendly bears: Great Big Papa Bear, Gentle Mama Bear, and Sweet Little Baby Bear.

One sunny morning, Mama Bear made sweet oat porridge for breakfast. Because it was too hot to eat, the three bears went for a pleasant morning walk while it cooled.

A curious little girl named Goldilocks came walking through the trees and saw their open cottage door.

Inside, she saw three bowls of porridge.
She tasted Papa Bear's porridge: "Too hot!"
She tasted Mama Bear's porridge: "Too cold!"
She tasted Baby Bear's porridge: "Just right!" And she ate it all up!

Next, Goldilocks tried the three chairs in the living room.
Papa Bear's chair was too hard.
Mama Bear's chair was too soft.
Baby Bear's chair was just right, but she rocked so happily that the little chair broke!

Feeling sleepy, Goldilocks went upstairs to the bedroom.
Papa Bear's bed was too high.
Mama Bear's bed was too soft.
Baby Bear's bed was just right, and she fell fast asleep.

When the three bears came home, Baby Bear squeaked: "Somebody has been sleeping in my bed—and here she is!"

Goldilocks woke up, saw the three bears, leaped out of bed, and ran straight home through the forest. From that day on, she always knocked first and respected others' homes!
