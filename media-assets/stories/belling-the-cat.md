# Belling the Cat
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

In an old wooden barn, a family of little brown mice lived in constant fear of a sly black cat who tiptoed silently across the hay bales.

"The cat moves so quietly that we never hear her coming!" squeaked one mouse.

The mice held a grand meeting under the floorboards to find a solution. Many mice made suggestions, but none seemed quite right.

Then, a young mouse stood up proudly. "I have a brilliant plan!" he announced. "We will tie a silver bell around the cat's neck with a blue ribbon! Whenever the cat walks, the bell will jingle: *tinkle, tinkle!* We will hear her coming and hide safely in our holes!"

All the young mice clapped their paws and cheered. "What a wonderful idea!"

Then, an old, wise mouse with grey whiskers stepped forward quietly.

"That is indeed a very clever plan, young friend," said the wise mouse. "There is only one question: Which of us is going to tie the bell on the cat?"

The room fell completely silent. Not a single mouse raised a paw.

**It is one thing to say what should be done, and quite another to do it.**
