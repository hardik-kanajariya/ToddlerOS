# The Boy Who Cried Wolf
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

High on a grassy hill near a sunny village, a young shepherd boy sat watching his flock of fluffy white sheep.

Day after day, the sheep munched on sweet clover, and the boy felt very bored. "Nothing exciting ever happens up here," he sighed.

To stir up some excitement, the boy took a deep breath and ran down toward the village, shouting: "Wolf! Wolf! A wolf is chasing the sheep!"

The villagers dropped their tools and hurried up the hill to help. But when they arrived, there was no wolf—only the sheep chewing grass and the boy laughing gleefully.

"It was just a joke!" chuckled the boy.

The villagers shook their heads. "Never make jokes about danger," they warned gently.

The next week, the boy shouted "Wolf!" once again, and once again the villagers rushed up to find only laughter.

Then, on a quiet afternoon, a real grey wolf crept out from the forest shadows. Frightened, the boy yelled as loudly as he could: "Wolf! Wolf! Please help, a real wolf is here!"

In the village, the people heard the shouts, but they believed it was just another trick. Nobody came up the hill.

The boy hid safely in a tree, but he was very sad. When the villagers finally came looking for him, the boy had learned a timeless truth:
**Nobody believes a liar, even when they speak the truth.**
