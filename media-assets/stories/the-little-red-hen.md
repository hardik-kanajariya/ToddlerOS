# The Little Red Hen
*A Traditional Folk Tale*

A busy Little Red Hen lived in a sunny farmyard with a sleepy dog, a lazy cat, and a noisy duck.

One morning, the Little Red Hen found a handful of golden wheat grains on the path.

"Who will help me plant this wheat?" she asked.
"Not I," barked the dog, resting under the porch.
"Not I," purred the cat, dozing in the sun.
"Not I," quacked the duck, floating on the pond.

"Then I will do it myself," said the Little Red Hen. And she did!

When the wheat grew tall and golden, she asked: "Who will help me harvest the wheat?"
"Not I," said the dog.
"Not I," said the cat.
"Not I," said the duck.
"Then I will do it myself," she said.

When the wheat was ground into fine white flour, she baked a warm, golden loaf of bread. Its delicious aroma drifted across the farmyard.

"Who will help me eat this fresh warm bread?" called the Little Red Hen.
"I will!" barked the dog.
"I will!" purred the cat.
"I will!" quacked the duck.

"Oh no," smiled the Little Red Hen. "My little chicks and I planted, watered, harvested, and baked it. We will share it together!" And they enjoyed every warm, buttery crumb.
