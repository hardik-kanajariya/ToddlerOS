# The Tale of Benjamin Bunny
*By Beatrix Potter (1904)*

One sunny morning, little Benjamin Bunny sat upon a green bank and listened to the robins singing in the lane.

He saw his cousin, Peter Rabbit, walking alone, wrapped in a red cotton handkerchief.

"Peter," asked Benjamin, "where are your clothes?"

"Mr. McGregor has them," whispered Peter. "I left my little blue jacket and shoes in his garden."

Benjamin took Peter by the hand. "Do not worry, cousin. We will go together and get your jacket back!"

They crept through the woods and hopped onto the wall above the garden. There, hanging upon a scarecrow, were Peter's blue jacket and brown shoes.

Quietly and carefully, Benjamin helped Peter untie his jacket. They collected a bundle of fresh sweet onions to bring home to their aunt.

With joyful hearts, the two brave little bunnies scampered back through the woods, hand in hand, safe and sound.
