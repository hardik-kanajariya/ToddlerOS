# The Tale of Mrs. Tiggy-Winkle
*By Beatrix Potter (1905)*

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucie, who lived at a farm in the green hills. Lucie was always losing her pocket-handkerchiefs.

One day she walked up the grassy path behind the farm and found a tiny wooden door tucked into the hillside.

Lucie knocked on the door: *tap, tap, tap!*

A gentle voice called, "Who's that?" Lucie opened the door and peeked into a cozy little kitchen with a crackling fire and the smell of warm irons.

There stood a tiny person with brown prickles under her clean white apron—it was Mrs. Tiggy-Winkle, the woodland washerwoman!

"Good morning, Lucie!" smiled Mrs. Tiggy-Winkle. "I am ironing clothes for all the forest animals! Here are the robin's red waistcoat, and the lambs' woolly mittens, and look—here are your lost handkerchiefs, all washed clean and pressed smooth!"

Lucie helped Mrs. Tiggy-Winkle fold the clean clothes into a straw basket. They had tea together, and Lucie walked home feeling very grateful for her wonderful new friend.
