# Vasu's Sunny Day in the Garden
*A ToddlerOS Original Story*

Good morning, Vasu! The golden sun peeked over the green hills and smiled brightly down upon the garden.

A warm, fresh breeze whispered through the tall oak trees: *swish, swish, swish!*

Vasu put on little blue boots and walked outside onto the soft green grass.
Under a big green leaf, a friendly little frog blinked his eyes and said: *"Ribbit, ribbit, hello Vasu!"*

Vasu giggled and waved: "Hello, little frog!"

Next, Vasu visited the strawberry patch. A fluffy bumblebee was buzzing gently around a sweet red blossom: *"Bzzzzz, bzzzzz, good morning Vasu!"*

High in the branches of the apple tree, a cheerful robin chirped a merry morning song.

A fluffy white cloud drifted across the bright blue sky. A gentle shower of soft raindrops fell for just a moment: *pitter, patter, pitter, patter.*

Then the sun shone through the raindrops, creating a giant sparkling rainbow right above the garden: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, and Purple!

Vasu looked up at the rainbow with bright, happy eyes.
"What a wonderful, sunny day!" smiled Vasu.
