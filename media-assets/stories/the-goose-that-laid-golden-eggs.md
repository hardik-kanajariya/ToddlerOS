# The Goose That Laid the Golden Eggs
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

Once upon a time, a humble farmer and his wife lived on a quiet cottage farm with a few chickens and a special white goose.

One morning, when the farmer went out to collect eggs, he found something glittering brightly in the straw nest. He picked it up—it was heavy and made of solid, sparkling gold!

Every morning after that, the goose laid one beautiful golden egg.

The farmer and his wife sold the eggs and became very wealthy. They bought fine clothes, a beautiful house, and sweet treats.

However, as the farmer grew richer, he also grew impatient. "Why should we wait for just one egg each day?" he said to his wife. "Surely this goose must be filled with pure gold inside. If we take it all at once, we will be the richest people in the world!"

Greed had blinded the farmer. He went out to the barn and discovered to his sorrow that the goose was an ordinary goose inside.

Now the wonderful gift was gone forever. The farmer wept and realized that by wanting too much all at once, he had lost the precious treasure he already had.

**Always appreciate the gifts of today.**
