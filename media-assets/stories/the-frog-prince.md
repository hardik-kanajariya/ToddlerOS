# The Frog Prince
*A Classic Brothers Grimm Fairy Tale*

In a kingdom far away, a young princess loved to play with a golden ball in the cool shade of the royal garden near a deep, clear well.

One afternoon, she threw the golden ball too high. It splashed into the deep water and sank out of sight! The princess wept bitterly.

A little green frog poked his head out of the lily pads: *ribbit, ribbit!*
"Why are you weeping, gentle princess?" asked the frog.

"My golden ball has fallen into the well!" she cried.

"I can dive down and retrieve it," said the frog, "if you promise to let me sit beside you at dinner and be your friend."

"I promise!" said the princess.

The frog dived deep, brought up the golden ball, and placed it upon the grass. The princess grabbed the ball and ran happily back to the palace, forgetting her promise.

That evening at dinner, a knock came at the palace door. The king listened to the little frog's story and said gently to his daughter: "A promise made must always be kept."

The princess welcomed the frog to the table, shared her sweet apples, and treated him with kindness.

Suddenly, a bright sparkle of magical light filled the room. The little frog transformed into a handsome prince! He had been enchanted, and only a kind promise kept could break the spell.
