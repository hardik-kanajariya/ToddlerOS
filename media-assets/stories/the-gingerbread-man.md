# The Gingerbread Man
*A Classic Children's Tale*

Once upon a time, a kind little old woman and a cheerful old man lived in a cottage near a forest.

One morning, the woman baked a special gingerbread boy. She gave him two shiny raisin eyes, a smiling cinnamon mouth, and three sweet sugar buttons down his coat.

When she opened the oven door, the gingerbread boy jumped right out onto the floor and ran out the front door, laughing!

The old woman and man called: "Stop, little gingerbread boy!"

But he just laughed and sang out as loud as he could:
*"Run, run, as fast as you can!*
*You can't catch me, I'm the Gingerbread Man!"*

He ran past a cow in the pasture, a horse in the meadow, and a sheep on the hill. All of them chased him, but nobody could run as fast as the Gingerbread Man!

Soon he reached a wide, flowing river. "Oh dear," he said, "I cannot swim or I will get soggy!"

A sly fox was waiting by the water's edge with a smile. "Hop onto my bushy tail, little friend, and I will carry you across safely!"

The gingerbread man hopped onto the fox's tail, and together they crossed the river to the green meadow on the other side.
