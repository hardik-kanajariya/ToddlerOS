# The Princess and the Pea
*By Hans Christian Andersen (1835)*

Once upon a time, there was a kind young prince who wished to marry a true princess. He traveled all over the world, but he could never be quite certain if the princesses he met were genuine.

One stormy night, thunder roared and lightning flashed across the sky. The rain poured down in torrents.

A quiet knock was heard at the castle gate: *tap, tap, tap.*

The old king went down and opened the door. Standing outside in the rain was a young maiden. The water dripped from her hair and down her velvet coat, but she carried herself with such grace and dignity. She said she was a true princess.

"We shall soon see about that," thought the old queen.

The queen went into the guest bedroom, took all the blankets off the bed, and placed one tiny green pea on the wooden slats. Then she piled twenty thick mattresses on top of the pea, and twenty soft feather beds on top of the mattresses!

There the princess slept for the night.

In the morning, the queen asked her: "How did you sleep, dear princess?"

"Oh, very poorly," replied the princess politely. "There was something terribly hard in the bed, and I am black and blue all over!"

Now everyone in the castle knew she was a true princess, for only a real princess could feel a tiny pea through twenty mattresses and twenty feather beds! The prince smiled with joy, and they lived happily ever after.
