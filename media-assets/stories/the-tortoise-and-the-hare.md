# The Tortoise and the Hare
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

Once upon a time, in a peaceful green meadow, lived a speedy little hare and a calm, steady tortoise.

The hare loved to boast about how quickly he could run. "Nobody in this forest is faster than me!" he cheered, leaping over rocks and flowers.

The gentle tortoise smiled and spoke softly. "You are very fast, little hare. But if you wish, I will race you to the big oak tree."

The hare laughed with delight. "A race with a tortoise? That will be fun!"

When the race began, the hare dashed ahead like the wind. He ran so far and so fast that soon the tortoise was far behind out of sight.

"I have plenty of time," thought the hare. "The sun is warm and the grass is soft. I will take a little nap under this shady tree."

The hare closed his eyes and fell fast asleep.

Meanwhile, step by step, plodding patiently along, the tortoise kept walking. He did not run, but he never stopped.

The sun moved across the blue sky. The tortoise passed the sleeping hare, passed the sparkling brook, and reached the great oak tree at the finish line!

When the hare woke up, the animals were cheering. The tortoise had won!

The hare learned an important lesson that sunny day:
**Slow and steady wins the race.**
