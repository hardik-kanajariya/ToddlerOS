# The Three Little Pigs
*A Traditional Fairy Tale*

Once upon a time, three little pigs set out into the big wide world to build homes of their own.

The first little pig was in a hurry to play. He built his house quickly out of golden straw.
The second little pig was also in a hurry. He built his house out of brown sticks from the woods.
The third little pig was patient and thoughtful. He worked hard all day, laying strong red bricks and mortar to build a sturdy home.

Soon, a big hungry wolf came to the straw house. "Little pig, little pig, let me come in!"
"Not by the hair of my chinny chin chin!" squealed the first pig.
"Then I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house down!"
The wolf blew down the straw house in one puff. The first pig ran as fast as his trotters could go to his brother's stick house.

The wolf followed and blew the stick house down, too! Both pigs ran to their brother's brick house and slammed the door.

The wolf arrived at the brick house: "I'll huff, and I'll puff, and I'll blow your house down!"
The wolf huffed, and he puffed, and he huffed again. But the brick house stood firm and strong!

The three little pigs sat safely by the warm fireplace, laughing and singing happily together.
