# The Country Mouse and the City Mouse
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

Country Mouse lived in a snug little hollow under an old oak tree in the countryside. He ate sweet wild berries, acorns, and crunchy sunflower seeds.

One day, his cousin, City Mouse, came to visit. "Dear cousin," said City Mouse, "your country food is very plain. Come to the city and see how mice dine in grand palaces!"

So they traveled together to a giant town mansion. In the grand dining hall, the table was covered with frosted cakes, yellow cheeses, honey buns, and grapes!

"Taste this yellow cheese!" whispered City Mouse proudly.

Just as Country Mouse took a delicious bite, the heavy wooden door burst open! In bounded a giant black cat with green eyes, followed by two barking dogs!

"Run!" cried City Mouse, and both mice squeezed into a tiny crack in the baseboard, their little hearts beating like drums.

They waited in the dark corner for hours until the house fell quiet again.

Country Mouse picked up his tiny straw walking stick and smiled at his cousin. "You may keep your rich cakes and grand halls, cousin. I prefer my quiet acorn and peace of mind under the country stars."
