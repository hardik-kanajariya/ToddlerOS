# The Ant and the Grasshopper
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

On a warm summer morning, a merry grasshopper hopped through the green field, singing cheerful songs and playing his fiddle under the sunshine.

Nearby, a tiny brown ant marched by, carrying a golden grain of wheat on his back. The grain was twice as heavy as the ant, but the ant walked forward with pride.

"Why work so hard on such a beautiful day?" called the grasshopper. "Come sing and dance with me!"

"Summer is wonderful," replied the ant, wiping his brow. "But winter will come with cold snow. I am gathering food now so my family will stay warm and fed."

"Winter is far away!" laughed the grasshopper, dancing away on the clover.

All through the summer and autumn, the ants gathered seeds and stored them inside their cozy underground pantry.

When winter arrived, a frosty blanket of white snow covered the fields. The icy wind blew through the trees. The grasshopper searched everywhere, but could not find a single blade of grass or seed.

Cold and hungry, the grasshopper knocked softly on the ant's little door. The kind ant welcomed him inside to the warm hearth and shared a bowl of sweet wheat porridge.

"Next summer," promised the grateful grasshopper, "I will sing for you, and I will help you carry the grain, too!"
