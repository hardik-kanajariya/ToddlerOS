# The Lion and the Mouse
*A Classic Aesop's Fable*

A mighty lion was sleeping peacefully under a warm acacia tree on the sunny savannah.

A tiny mouse scampered across the grass and accidentally ran across the lion's big nose. The lion woke up with a sleepy growl and scooped the little mouse up in his giant paw.

"Please, kind Lion!" squeaked the tiny mouse. "I did not mean to wake you! If you let me go, perhaps one day I can help you in return."

The big lion chuckled softly. "A tiny mouse help the king of beasts? That is very funny!" But because the lion had a kind heart, he opened his paw and set the mouse free.

A few weeks later, while walking through the tall grass, the lion stepped into a hunter's strong rope net. He pulled and pulled, but he could not break free. He let out a mighty roar that echoed across the plains.

The little mouse heard the roar and ran as fast as his little legs could carry him.

"Do not worry, dear friend!" said the mouse. With his sharp little teeth, the mouse nibbled and chewed at the thick ropes: *nibble, nibble, snip!*

Soon the ropes snapped open, and the lion stepped out, free and happy.

"Thank you, little mouse," smiled the great lion. "You were right: small friends can be the greatest helpers of all."
